# Help improve Own Your View

Use any language you are comfortable with. Short, specific feedback is enough; the prompts below are optional.

- What idea were you examining, and why?
- Which response helped, misunderstood you, pushed you toward a conclusion, or made the conversation harder?
- What did you keep, change, or remain uncertain about, and why? No change is also a useful observation.
- What would you prefer next time? You do not need to propose a fix.
- If available: skill version, AI product/model, and whether browsing was available. Write "unknown" if the interface does not show this.

Suggestions from people who have not tried it are welcome; please label them as untested suggestions. We also want failures: an irrelevant counterexample, a rewrite offered too early, invented evidence, unnecessary questions, or a stop request ignored.

Share only a minimal anonymized excerpt or a made-up example. Do not include credentials, student records, private work documents, identifying details, copyrighted book/article text, or a full conversation. Public issues are visible to others. The skill does not submit feedback automatically.

If you voluntarily try a similar situation with different conditions, tell us whether you could explain your own judgment without a suggested answer. You can skip or stop this exercise. Agreement with AI, satisfaction, or a single result does not establish improved reasoning.

When an issue is reproducible, we will consider a small change and a relevant regression check. Conflicting preferences may need different handling rather than another universal rule. We will ask before using someone else's feedback as a public example.
